Every line is a clue.

Move hex tiles into place and let every colorful line find its match.

Read the board

Triangle, circle, and hex patterns keep the puzzle language bright and easy to understand.

Chase stars

Relaxed challenge

Play offline, restart instantly, and solve each board at your own pace.
