Game Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 28, 2026

Twisty Hex Lines respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game.

Information We Collect

The core game is designed for offline play. Game progress, stars, settings, and level status may be stored locally on your device. The app template may also use advertising, analytics, and security SDKs that can process device identifiers, diagnostics, ad interaction data, and similar technical information as needed to provide ads, protect the app, and improve reliability.

How We Use Information

  • To save your local game progress and settings.
  • To provide and measure ads when advertising services are available.
  • To maintain app security, prevent abuse, and improve stability.
  • To respond to support requests.

Third-Party Services

The app may include third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, and security. These providers may process data according to their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Children's Privacy

Twisty Hex Lines is a casual puzzle game. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device unless you delete the app or clear its data. Support messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the request.

Your Choices

You can manage tracking permissions, advertising preferences, and app permissions in iOS Settings. You can also contact us for privacy questions.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
